In vitro drug–drug interaction risk assessment

Assess the DDI potential of an investigational drug from in vitro data and human exposure, and determine whether further evaluation by mechanistic modeling or a clinical DDI study is warranted, following ICH M12 Drug Interaction Studies (Step 4, May 2024; issued by FDA in August 2024).

Assessment workflow

Each tier can end the assessment. A result that clears a cut-off is recorded with its calculated value and the evaluation stops there; only an unresolved risk moves down. A mechanistic static model is optional — ICH M12 allows a move straight from the basic model to PBPK or to a clinical study.

1

Compound and physiological parameters

Applies to every dose level. Physiological values are the ICH M12 defaults for a 70 kg adult; the intestinal volume is fixed at 250 mL and normally stay as they are.

Set fu,p to 0.01 when a measured value below 1% cannot be shown to be reliable. Two values of Fg and fm are kept because the probe substrate differs: CYP3A uses midazolam, the remaining enzymes use a hypothetical sensitive substrate.

2

Dose level and exposure parameters

Enter the dose and the steady-state Cmax for up to five dose levels. Everything below is derived from these two rows.

3

CYP reversible inhibition — basic model

Cmax,u / Ki,u ≥ 0.02hepatic(Dose / 250 mL) / Ki,u ≥ 10intestinal CYP3A

Ki = IC50 / 2  ·  Ki,u = Ki × fu,mic

An IC50 entered as “> 50” is treated as 50, which is the conservative reading.

4

CYP time-dependent inhibition — basic model

R2 = (kobs + kdeg) / kdeg ≥ 1.25

kobs = kinact × 5 Cmax,u / (KI,u + 5 Cmax,u)

ICH M12 uses 5 × Cmax,u here; the superseded FDA 2020 guidance used 50 ×. Each enzyme uses its own kdeg.

5

CYP inhibition — mechanistic static model

AUCR ≥ 1.25

AUCR = 1 / [(Ah·Bh)·fm + (1 − fm)] × 1 / [(Ag·Bg)·(1 − Fg) + Fg]

CYP3A (midazolam) uses the CYP3A fm and Fg. CYP3A (testosterone) follows the workbook and uses the other-CYP fm and Fg, so its intestinal factor is 1. For the remaining enzymes Ag = Bg = 1 and Fg = 1.

6

CYP induction — basic model

R3 ≤ 0.8

R3 = 1 / (1 + d · Emax · 10 Cmax,u / (EC50,u + 10 Cmax,u))  ·  RIS = Emax · Cmax,u / (EC50,u + Cmax,u)

Enter Emax as the fitted maximum induction effect. If your model is E = 1 + Emax·C/(EC50 + C), that is the fold change minus one — check the report before entering it. RIS has no fixed cut-off; convert it through the calibration curve of the hepatocyte batch.

7

CYP induction — mechanistic static model

AUCR ≤ 0.8

AUCR = 1 / [Ch·fm + (1 − fm)] × 1 / [Cg·(1 − Fg) + Fg]  ·  Ch = 1 + d·Emax·[I]h / ([I]h + EC50,u)  ·  Cg = 1 + d·Emax·[I]g / ([I]g + EC50,u)

8

Transporter inhibition — basic model

R ≥ 10P-gp, BCRP (intestinal)R ≥ 0.1OATP1B1, OATP1B3R ≥ 0.1OAT1, OAT3, OCT2R ≥ 0.02MATE1, MATE2-K

R = (Dose / 250 mL) / IC50,u intestinal  ·  R = [I]h / IC50,u OATP  ·  R = Cmax,u / IC50,u renal

IC50,u defaults to the measured IC50. Overwrite it with IC50 × fu,inc where non-specific binding was determined. Intestinal criteria apply to oral dosing only.

9

Overall assessment

Every enzyme and transporter that exceeds its cut-off at one or more dose levels, grouped by mechanism and by the model that flagged it, with the next step for each group.
